South African reading

A synthesis of published evidence, not a combined survey result
High demand for feedback, mentorship and practical learning sits alongside a difficult early-career labour market.
  • 98% of South African Gen Z respondents value formal training.
  • 97% value on-the-job learning and 97% see mentorship as essential.
  • Stats SA shows that younger workers enter this environment while facing unusually high unemployment and NEET rates.
The figures come from separate sources and should be read as context, not as proof of causation.

1

Employer concern is measurable

Motivation, professionalism, communication, feedback and problem-solving all appear in the employer survey as reasons recent graduate hires did not work out.

2

Demand for development is also measurable

South African Gen Z respondents report very high interest in training, on-the-job learning, mentorship and feedback.

3

The practical question is what closes the gap

Clear onboarding, frequent feedback, mentoring and visible development pathways are reasonable management actions to test against internal retention and performance data.
